# Organisation brief

[Home](README.md)

For this assignment, consider Rabobank's IT support for payments, online and mobile banking, branch operations, core banking, infrastructure, and employee devices.

## Who handles the work?

| Team | Role |
|---|---|
| Central Service Desk | Receives incidents, classifies them, resolves known issues, and routes other work. |
| Application and Infrastructure Triage | Identifies the right team when ownership is unclear. |
| Specialist support teams | Investigate and restore services in their areas, including payments, core banking, networks, and workplace devices. |
| Incident Management Office | Coordinates escalation and major incidents, and owns priority decisions. |
| Customer Case Desk | Communicates with users about ongoing incidents. |
| Supplier liaison teams | Coordinate work with external application and hardware suppliers. |

## Arrangements described in the proposal

- Human support operates Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00, Europe/Amsterdam.
- No human on-call arrangement is specified. Monitoring automation runs continuously.
- Incident targets continue counting outside support hours.
- Supplier response commitments are not specified.

**These arrangements are open to challenge.** If something essential is missing for an organisation like this, please say so. Missing information is itself useful feedback.
