# Optional discussion starter

[Home](README.md)

A payments service becomes unavailable across several branches just before support hours end on Friday. The desk has logged the incident, but the specialist team has not yet responded.

**In your experience, what would need to happen next—and what do these models fail to provide for?**

You might consider who takes ownership, how help is mobilised, and what the business needs to know. You do not need to calculate a deadline or demonstrate the correct reading of the rules.

Feel free to use a different situation from your own experience if it exposes a more important gap. Include any observations in your feedback; no separate scenario answer is required.
