Start with **priority and escalation**. Then look at support hours and service targets to see whether they support the way work would actually happen. Focus on the areas where you have the most experience.

| Model | Where to focus |
|---|---|
| [Priority and classification](models/classification-model.md) | Impact, urgency, the priority matrix, and overrides |
| [Escalation](models/escalation-model.md) | Functional and hierarchical escalation; skim repeated routing rows |
| [Support calendar](models/support-calendar-model.md) | Coverage and after-hours arrangements |
| [Service targets](models/sla-ola-uc-model.md) | Response targets, clock rules, and breach handling |

These are working drafts. You do not need to review every row or correct the writing.

## What we want to learn

- **What is missing?** Think of situations, responsibilities, or decisions you regularly encounter that these models do not cover.
- **What would fail in practice?** Where would people become stuck, work around the rules, or need an exception?
- **What would you add or change?** Explain the practical arrangement that would make the model usable.

Use examples from your experience, without naming customers or sharing confidential details. You can also flag unrealistic assumptions in the organisation brief.
