# ITSM expert profiles

We are looking for practitioners who can review proposed IT service management models and explain what is missing, unrealistic, or likely to fail in everyday operations. We also want contributors who can describe complex work episodes spanning teams, approvals, suppliers, or systems.

## Profiles we are looking for

| Profile | Main contribution | Relevant experience |
|---|---|---|
| ServiceNow ITSM Functional Consultant / Business Process Consultant | Identify gaps in operating rules, responsibilities, exceptions, and handoffs | Process workshops, requirements, ITSM process design, user acceptance testing, and post-launch support |
| ServiceNow ITSM Technical Lead / Senior Technical Consultant | Explain how workflows and integrations behave when work becomes complicated | ITSM implementation, integration design, automation, technical delivery, and production troubleshooting |
| ServiceNow Developer / Administrator | Bring detailed knowledge of live records, permissions, configuration, and everyday workflow failures | Hands-on configuration or development, platform support, incident investigation, and direct work with users or process owners |

Titles vary across employers. Assess the work a person has done rather than relying on their title, years of experience, or certifications alone.

## 1. ServiceNow ITSM Functional Consultant / Business Process Consultant

**Also search for:** ITSM Process Consultant, ServiceNow Functional Lead, ServiceNow Business Analyst, ServiceNow Techno-functional Consultant.

Look for someone who has worked with process owners and operational teams to define Incident, Problem, Change, or Request workflows. Strong candidates can explain how requirements changed when real exceptions emerged during workshops, testing, or daily use.

Useful experience includes:

- Defining ownership, escalation, approval, and fulfilment rules with several teams.
- Identifying gaps between documented processes and how people actually work.
- Writing practical requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Supporting user acceptance testing and seeing what happened after launch.

**Ask them to contribute:** missing decisions and unrealistic assumptions in the models, plus examples of work that required negotiation or exceptions across teams.

**Screening question:** “Tell us about a requirement that looked straightforward until you discussed exceptions with the people doing the work. What changed, and why?”

## 2. ServiceNow ITSM Technical Lead / Senior Technical Consultant

**Also search for:** ServiceNow Technical Consultant, ServiceNow Implementation Lead, ServiceNow Development Lead, ServiceNow Integration Lead.

Look for someone who has translated business requirements into working ITSM solutions and remained involved in troubleshooting or supporting those solutions. Prefer experience across multiple workflows or enterprise integrations.

Useful experience includes:

- Designing and implementing incident, change, request, or approval workflows.
- Connecting ServiceNow with identity, monitoring, asset, supplier, or other enterprise systems.
- Handling failed automation, partial fulfilment, conflicting updates, and recovery.
- Explaining technical constraints and tradeoffs to process owners and operational teams.

**Ask them to contribute:** workflow failure paths, missing dependencies, realistic automation boundaries, and episodes where recovery required coordination across systems and teams.

**Screening question:** “Describe work that crossed several teams or systems and became stuck when one part failed. How was it detected, who took ownership, and how was completion verified?”

## 3. ServiceNow Developer / Administrator

**Also search for:** ServiceNow Application Developer, ServiceNow System Administrator, ServiceNow Platform Support Engineer, ServiceNow Support Consultant.

Look for someone with direct exposure to live ITSM use. Developers and administrators are especially useful when they investigate user-reported problems, maintain workflows, and understand why teams work around the configured process.

Useful experience includes:

- Configuring or supporting assignment rules, approvals, notifications, service targets, forms, and access permissions.
- Investigating stuck tasks, incorrect routing, failed integrations, or unexpected record states.
- Supporting releases, upgrades, or regression testing.
- Working directly with service desk staff, fulfilment teams, or process owners to resolve practical issues.

**Ask them to contribute:** concrete operational edge cases, the information needed to diagnose them, actions taken, and evidence that the issue was resolved.

**Screening question:** “Tell us about a recurring issue users reported that the configured workflow did not handle well. What did users do instead, and what did you change?”

## What makes a strong contributor?

- Personally participated in the work they describe.
- Can explain the sequence, people involved, missing information, decisions, and outcome.
- Distinguishes an organisation's chosen policy from platform behaviour or a local customisation.
- Can challenge a proposed rule and suggest a practical alternative.
- Shares anonymised examples without customer names, confidential documents, or identifiable records.

For developers and administrators, narrow implementation work can still be valuable, but match the assignment to their actual exposure. Certifications are supporting evidence, not a substitute for practical examples.

## Contribution formats

**Model review — 30–60 minutes:** Read a short organisation brief and a focused set of models. Identify a few meaningful gaps, explain what happens in practice, and suggest changes. See the [review assignment](README.md).

**Work episode interview — 45–60 minutes:** Walk through one complex case. Cover the trigger, initial information, roles, handoffs, decisions, setbacks, and evidence of completion. This is a separate session from model review.
