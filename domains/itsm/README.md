# Expert review: What is missing from everyday IT operations?

**Organisation:** Rabobank · **Time:** 30–60 minutes

We would like your practical judgement on proposed incident-management rules. Given the organisation below, **what is missing, unrealistic, or likely to fail in day-to-day work?**

Draw on your experience. Please challenge the assumptions as well as the rules, and suggest what a workable approach would need.

## Start here

1. [Organisation brief](01-organisation-context.md) — 5 minutes.
2. [Models to review](02-review-materials.md) — 20–30 minutes.
3. [Share your feedback](04-feedback-template.md) — 10–15 minutes.

If useful, use the [optional scenario](03-scenario.md) to think through a concrete case.

A few specific observations are enough. An example of what happens in practice and what you would add is especially valuable. Stop at 60 minutes; tell us which areas you covered.
